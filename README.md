 🚨 LIFEQR

 Emergency Identity & Rapid Contact System

> **When they can't speak, LIFEQR speaks for them.**

LIFEQR is a smart QR-based emergency identity system designed to help people access essential emergency information when a person is unable to communicate during an accident or emergency.

A unique QR code can be placed on a helmet, ID card, vehicle, bag, bracelet, or phone. When the QR code is scanned, the emergency profile provides essential information and a way to contact the person's trusted emergency contact.

---

🎯 Problem Statement

During an accident or emergency, a person may be unconscious, injured, or unable to communicate.

In such situations, people nearby may not know:

- Who the person is
- Their blood group
- Their allergies
- Important medical information
- Whom to contact

This lack of information can cause delays during a critical situation.

---

💡 Our Solution

LIFEQR provides every user with a unique digital emergency identity.

The user creates an emergency profile and receives a unique QR code. The QR can be attached to a helmet, ID card, vehicle, bag, bracelet, or phone.

When someone finds the person during an emergency, they can scan the QR code and quickly access the essential emergency information and contact the trusted person.

-> Simple Flow

"Create Profile → Generate QR → Place QR → Scan During Emergency → View Information → Contact Trusted Person"

---

🔄 How LIFEQR Works

1. Create Profile
The user enters their essential emergency information.

2. Generate QR
LIFEQR creates a unique QR code for the profile.

3. Attach QR
The QR can be printed or placed on a helmet, ID card, phone, vehicle, bag, or wearable item.

4. Emergency Occurs
If the person cannot communicate, another person can scan the QR.

5. Emergency Profile Opens
The scanner can view the essential emergency information.

6. Contact Trusted Person
The scanner can use the emergency contact option to reach the registered contact.

Users should only provide information they are comfortable making available through an emergency profile.
